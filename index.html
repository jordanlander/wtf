BEFORE YOU SHIP

A few useful details.

Where is Wiley Trucking Fleet based?

Wiley Trucking Fleet is based in Erie, Pennsylvania, and offers coast-to-coast freight transportation across the United States. Contact dispatch to discuss availability for your specific lane.

What information do I need for a freight quote?

Have your pickup and delivery ZIP codes, commodity, total weight, and date and time windows ready. Include your pallet count when applicable, service requirements, and contact details.

Can I request power-only or dedicated service?

Yes. Choose power-only, dedicated, or subcontract service in the quote form and include the lane and equipment details. Dispatch will discuss availability and requirements with you.

Does the form book my pickup?

No. The form sends a freight quote inquiry. Rates, availability, and pickup arrangements need to be confirmed with dispatch. For pickup scheduling, call (814) 731-8870.
